# Physical Specifications
This document will house the numerical values of dimensional measurements for the case, film, and PCB -- all components of the cartridge. All measurements were measured using a digital caliper with a **resolution** of 0.1mm/0.01" and an **accuracy** of $\pm$ 0.2mm/0.01".

## Case Components
### Carousel
* Diameter of big nubs = 2.2mm
* Diameter of small nubs = 1.9mm
> ![Note]
> Carousel-film mounting becomes arbitrary in a modded cartrisge due to its lack of essential functionality interfacing with the projector. As long as the film is fixed to the carousel in the right position, and turns with the carousel, the mounting method can be altered. An aspect of the official mounting method I would like to retain is the forced directionality the mounts impose on the film ensuring it is oriented and faced correctly.
