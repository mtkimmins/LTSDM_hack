# Physical Specifications
This document will house the numerical values of dimensional measurements for the case, film, and PCB -- all components of the cartridge. All measurements were measured using a digital caliper with a **resolution** of 0.1mm/0.01" and an **accuracy** of $\pm$ 0.2mm/0.01".

## Case Components
### Carousel
* Diameter of big nubs = 2.2mm
* Diameter of small nubs = 1.9mm
* Height of all nubs (top of nub to back of disc) = 2.0mm
* Total diameter = 38.7mm
* Center gear diameter = 11.9mm
* Central hole in gear head depth = 1.4mm
* Film window width (ea) = 5.0mm
* Film window height (ea) = 6.0mm
* Inner central hole diameter = 3.2mm
* Center lip thickness = 0.9mm
* Carousel disc thickness (uniform) = 1.0mm
* Outer central hole diameter = 9.5mm
* Bottom of frame-bottom of frame diameter = 22.7mm
* Margin thickness between window outer top->edge = 1.4mm
* Depth of outer central hole = 2.3mm
* Depth of inner central hole = 3.0mm
* Depth of black window = 0.4mm
* Margin thickness between black window and bottom of film window = 0.6mm
* Height from back of disc (no nub) to top of gear teeth = 4.7mm
* Number of gear teeth = 12
* Number of film windows = 12
> [!Note]
> Carousel-film mounting becomes arbitrary in a modded cartridge due to its lack of interfacing with the projector making it functionally non-critical. As long as the film is fixed to the carousel in the right position, and turns with the carousel, the mounting method can be altered.
>
> An aspect of the official mounting method I would like to retain is the forced directionality the mounts impose on the film ensuring it is oriented and faced correctly. We also must ensure that the black window aligns with the first frame of the film too.
>
> Film windows are spaced evenly along the radius of the carousel.
> Gear teeth are centrally aligned with their nearest film window.

## Film Components
### Film Disc (Both Halves)
> [!Note]
> In official cartridges the film disc is cut in half and mounted separately onto the same carousel. It is unclear as the the purpose of the division, but it can be speculated that the mass production process favoured it.
* Thickness (uniform across whole film) = 0.1mm
* Film diameter = 38mm
> [!Note]
> Film center hole edge borders on the outside of the carousel's central lip.
> Film background is all black except for the transparent arrows indicating rotation direction for each half
